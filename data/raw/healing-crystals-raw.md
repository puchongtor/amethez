## BOOK STRUCTURE & PROPERTIES BOX FORMAT

Each crystal entry includes a **Properties box** with these fields:
- **Type:** mineral family/group
- **Colour:** colors/variations
- **Zodiac Sign:** associated astrological sign(s)
- **Planet:** ruling planet
- **Element:** Fire/Water/Earth/Air
- **Candle Colour:** for ritual/healing use
- **Guardian Angel:** associated angel
- **Chakra:** primary chakra(s)
- **Herbs/Incenses/Oils:** associated botanicals
- **Flowers:** associated flower
- **Associated Crystals:** complementary stones
- **See also:** cross-references

Plus sections: Healing uses, Divinatory meaning, Children/Animals, Psychic/magical properties, Recharging method
