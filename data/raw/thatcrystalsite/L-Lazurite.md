Lazurite meaning
Lazurite is the blue component of Lapis Lazuli, which is a Lazurite-Calcite-Pyrite rock.
When working or meditating with Lapis Lazuli, it can bring matters more clearly to the mind. It is one of the most powerful stones and should be used with care. The ancient Egyptians used Lapis Lazuli as a symbol of Truth.
