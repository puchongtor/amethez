Sources of Atlantasite
Atlantasite is a relatively new mineral find. The purple and green combination only occurs in a small hilltop called "Stichtite Hill" which is in Zeehan, Tasmania. Atlantasite is a Magnesium Chromate (stitchtite)associated with theserpentine host rock.
