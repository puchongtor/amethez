Russian Rainbow Pyrite meaning and properties
This recently discovered form of pyrite is found along the banks of the Volga River in central Russia.
It occurs as a drusy blanket on the matrix. Drusy is a fine layer of miniature crystals that coats a matrix. This delightful mineral shimmers with all the colors of the rainbow!

Pyrite meaning
The resemblance of pyrite to gold has made it a traditional symbol for money and good luck. It's sunny golden color associates it with the sun, and with fortification and strengthening of the mind. The name comes from the Greek word meaning "a stone which strikes fire".
