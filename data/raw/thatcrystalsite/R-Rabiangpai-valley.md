Map
Only 70 kilometers from Rungsit ,Driving along  rungsit-Nakorn Nayok Road and turn left to Nakorn Nayok and get 3 Kilometers, you can get 2 traffic lights. After  that you should drive in the left of road and go to Bang Wang-lee. Get  13 Kilometers ,you could  see Sai-Thong Reservoir in this road and you will meet  the entrance of Rabiangprai valley resort  which located on Sai-Thong Reservoir.
