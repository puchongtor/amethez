Calcite gemstone meaning
Calcite is a powerful amplifier and cleanser of various types of energies. This transparent crystal can be found in a variety of colors and forms. includingclear, green, pink, orange and blue.
All calcites emanate a gentle energy - cleansing, revitalizing. Calcite opens energy channels.
