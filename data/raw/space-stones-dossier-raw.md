## 2. THAI TEKTITE — สะเก็ดดาวไทย / อุลกมณี

> Western books rarely name “Thai tektite” as a separate chapter.  
> Scientifically Thai pieces belong to the **Australasian tektite strewn field** (Indochina–Australia), same family as black tektites discussed under **Tektite** in Book of Stones / Crystal Healer.  
> Local Thai trade names: **สะเก็ดดาวไทย**, **อุลกมณี**, sometimes grouped with tektite/meteorite lore.

### 2.1 Scientific / identity notes (for encyclopedia accuracy)
- **Family:** Tektite (impact glass), not a meteorite fragment itself
- **Colour (typical Thai):** black / dark brown-black; shapes: splash-form, teardrop, disk, irregular
- **Strewn field:** Australasian — Thailand, Laos, Cambodia, Vietnam, Philippines, Australia, etc.
- **Composition theme:** high SiO₂ glass (same broad family as other tektites; Moldavite is the green Czech exception)
- **Relation to Moldavite:** same *class* of impact glass, different impact event / locality / colour / lore

### 2.2 Metaphysical (from general TEKTITE entries — apply carefully to Thai pieces)
From Book of Stones (Tektite pp.393–395) + thatcrystalsite tektite lore + Permutt:
- **Keywords:** ET connection, telepathy, raise vibration, good luck / power / magic (thatcrystal)
- **Element:** Storm
- **Chakras:** All; black Indochina types described as Earthstar → Soul Star flow (Ahsian)
- **Uses:** third-eye sleep/meditation; heighten aura & psychic sensitivity; amplify Moldavite / Libyan Gold / Tibetan Tektite
- **Pairings:** Lapis, Quartz, Jasper, Chalcedony, Opal; ground with Petrified Wood
- **Lore names worldwide:** China Thundergod inkstone; Aboriginal *Maban*; India Krishna gem / *agni mani*; Tibet Shambhala stone
