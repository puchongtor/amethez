Rather than placing your crystals randomly, you can place them in a specific pattern known as a crystal grid. The purpose of this is to alter the flow of energy towards an object, house, person, or another crystal. For example, you can arrange a grid of quartz crystal points around your bed to enhance well-being when you are in bed. Ifthe crystals point towards your bed, they increase the energy flow to the bed. If this feels uncomfortable, you can point the crystals away from the bed to release energy. If you are treating yourself or a friend for a particular ailment or problem (see Crystal Remedies, pages 104—139), you can arrange the crystals you are using in a grid around your body.
