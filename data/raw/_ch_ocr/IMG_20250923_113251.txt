EMOTIONAL GROUNDING When you are grounded, energy moves freely through your body and any excess flows away from you into the earth. The short-term effect of not being grounded is a feeling of uneasiness which many people describe as nervousness or restlessness. The longer-term effects of being ungrounded are an imbalance in your subtle energy that can lead to burn- out — when you feel you can simply no longer cope — or health problems.
