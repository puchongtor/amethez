## p.6–11 — INTRODUCING CRYSTALS
- Crystals used by medicine people/shamans for thousands of years; recorded 5,000+ years in Chinese traditional medicine, Ayurvedic texts, Bible (200+ crystal references)
- Found in prehistoric graves (Olmec, Egyptian pharaohs)
- Theophrastus *Peri Lithon* (On Stones, 372–287 BCE): taxonomy of gems — origin, physical properties, magical/healing powers; modern science kept physical taxonomy, dropped healing
- **What are crystals:** natural mineral solids with precise atomic arrangement; identified by color + Mohs hardness
- **How crystals work:** piezoelectric + pyroelectric effects; heat/electricity/focusing energy; placebo; “magic” = beyond current understanding
- Science: crystals vibrate, hold heat/electricity, focus light (watches, lasers); quartz increases frequency of light; quantum physics may explain more
- **Crystals in action exercises:** (1) clear quartz point 2–5cm from palm, clockwise circles — see light point move; (2) friend holds quartz near one palm — notice temperature/weight/itch differences
- **Crystal healing:** crystals channel, focus, store, transmit, transmute energy; speed natural processes (not do impossible); e.g. carnelian for colds — symptoms may worsen ~12h then resolve faster
- Ageing: crystals help body repair faster when natural repair slows
- Mental/emotional/spiritual: accelerate growth; sitting with quartz 10 min centers mind
- Work *with* crystals as “Stone People” / friends, don’t “use” them
- Book structure: Ch.1–2 principles & methods → Crystal Finder by color (~250 crystals) → Remedies (physical/emotional/spiritual/lifestyle)

### Preparing your space + programming (p.18–19)

- Ready space before meditation, chakra work, or remedy protocols
